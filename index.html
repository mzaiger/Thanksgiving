Thanksgiving Cooking Schedule & Timeline

Follow this step-by-step master timetable to make sure all 9 dishes land warm and fresh on the table at the exact same time.

3d
3 to 4 Days Before (Sunday–Monday)
  • Move frozen Butterball Turkey from freezer to bottom shelf of refrigerator to thaw safely: about 24 hours per 4–5 lbs (3 days for a 12 lb bird, 4 days for 16 lbs).
  • Verify all 26 Walmart basket items are present and logged in your checklist.
1d
Day Before (Wednesday Evening)
  • Bake the Traditional Pumpkin Pie with Great Value Pure Pumpkin & Pie Crusts. Chill in fridge overnight.
  • Cook the Fresh Cranberry Sauce (Jam) in a 3-quart stainless steel saucepan on the stovetop; let cool and refrigerate so it thickens.
  • Peel and cube russet potatoes; submerge in cold water in fridge to prevent browning.
AM
Thanksgiving Morning (4 Hours Before Feast)
  • Preheat oven to 325°F. Remove giblets/neck from Butterball Turkey, pat skin dry, and place in roasting pan.
  • Put Turkey in oven to roast (takes ~3 to 3.5 hours for a 12–14 lb bird).
  • Boil cubed sweet potatoes in a 5–6 quart pot for the Sweet Potato Marshmallow Casserole; mash with butter into the baking dish (marshmallows go on right before baking).
2h
2 Hours Before Serving
  • Boil prepped russet potatoes in the 8-quart stockpot and mash with milk/butter (warm them in the 1-quart saucepan). Keep covered on warm setting or double boiler.
  • Assemble Green Bean Casserole (drained green beans, mushroom soup, milk, half the fried onions).
  • Mix the JIFFY Corn Casserole batter and pour it into the baking dish.
1h
Final Hour (The Push!)
  • Remove Turkey when the thickest part of the breast and innermost thigh read 165°F. Cover loosely with foil to rest for 20 minutes (crucial for juicy meat!).
  • Set oven to 350°F. Put the JIFFY Corn Casserole in first (45–55 min), then the Green Bean Casserole about 30 minutes before serving and the Sweet Potato Casserole about 25 minutes before.
  • Make the Stove Top Stuffing in a 3–4 quart covered saucepan (5 minutes). Cook the Great Value Brown Gravy packets in a 2-quart saucepan over medium heat (1 cup cold water each, simmer 1 minute).
  • Bake the Brown & Serve Rolls last, following the package directions (they may want a hotter oven than the casseroles).
  • Carve turkey, scoop side dishes, top pie with Great Value Whipped Topping, and serve!

Burner plan: Cranberry sauce goes on one burner the day before, and the yams take one in the morning. The busy stretch is the last hour, when the mashed potatoes (covered, on low), the stuffing, and the gravy are on at the same time.
