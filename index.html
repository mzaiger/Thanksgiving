Thanksgiving Cooking Schedule & Timeline

Follow this step-by-step master timetable to make sure all 8 dishes land warm and fresh on the table at the exact same time.

📅 3 Days Before (Tuesday)

  • Move frozen Butterball Turkey from freezer to bottom shelf of refrigerator to thaw safely (needs 3 days).
  • Verify all 26 Walmart basket items + extra staples (including Sugar) are present.

📅 Day Before (Wednesday Evening)

  • Bake the Traditional Pumpkin Pie. Chill in fridge overnight.
  • Cook the Fresh Cranberry Sauce on stovetop (using your extra Sugar); let cool and refrigerate so it thickens into a rich jam.
  • Peel and cube russet potatoes; submerge in cold water in fridge.

🌅 Thanksgiving Morning (4 Hours Before Feast)

  • Preheat oven to 325°F. Prep and roast Turkey (~3 to 3.5 hours).
  • Boil sweet potatoes for Sweet Potato Marshmallow Casserole; mash into baking dish.

⏰ 2 Hours Before Serving

  • Boil prepped russet potatoes and mash with milk/butter.
  • Assemble Green Bean Casserole and JIFFY Corn Casserole.

🔥 Final 45 Minutes (The Push!)

  • Remove Turkey at 165°F. Cover loosely with foil to rest.
  • Increase oven temp to 350°F–375°F. Bake casseroles side-by-side.
  • Prepare Stove Top Stuffing and whisk Brown Gravy.
  • Heat Great Value Dinner Rolls (7-10 mins).
  • Carve turkey, scoop sides, top pie with Whipped Topping, and serve!
