Thanksgiving Cooking Schedule & Timeline

Follow this step-by-step master timetable to make sure all 8 dishes land warm and fresh on the table at the exact same time.

3d
3 Days Before (Tuesday)
  • Move frozen Butterball Turkey from freezer to bottom shelf of refrigerator to thaw safely (needs 3 days).
  • Verify all 26 Walmart basket items are present and logged in your checklist.
1d
Day Before (Wednesday Evening)
  • Bake the Traditional Pumpkin Pie with Great Value Pure Pumpkin & Pie Crusts. Chill in fridge overnight.
  • Cook the Fresh Cranberry Sauce on stovetop; let cool and refrigerate so it thickens into a rich jam.
  • Peel and cube russet potatoes; submerge in cold water in fridge to prevent browning.
AM
Thanksgiving Morning (4 Hours Before Feast)
  • Preheat oven to 325°F. Remove giblets/neck from Butterball Turkey, pat skin dry, and place in roasting pan.
  • Put Turkey in oven to roast (takes ~3 to 3.5 hours for a 12–14 lb bird).
  • Boil sweet potatoes for Sweet Potato Marshmallow Casserole; mash into baking dish.
2h
2 Hours Before Serving
  • Boil prepped russet potatoes and mash with milk/butter. Keep covered on warm setting or double boiler.
  • Assemble Green Bean Casserole (drained cut green beans, mushroom soup, fried onions).
  • Mix JIFFY Corn Casserole or batter into baking dish.
45m
Final 45 Minutes (The Push!)
  • Remove Turkey when internal temp reaches 165°F. Cover loosely with foil to rest for 20 minutes (crucial for juicy meat!).
  • Increase oven temp to 350°F–375°F. Bake Green Bean Casserole, Sweet Potato Marshmallow Casserole, and JIFFY Corn Casserole side-by-side.
  • Prepare Stove Top Stuffing on stovetop. Whisk Great Value Brown Gravy packets in saucepan over medium heat.
  • Pop Great Value Dinner Rolls into oven for final 7–10 minutes to heat up.
  • Carve turkey, scoop side dishes, top pie with Great Value Whipped Topping, and serve!
